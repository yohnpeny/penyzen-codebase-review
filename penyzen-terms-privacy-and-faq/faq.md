# Policy FAQ — Penyzen

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 4, 2026

This FAQ answers common questions about Penyzen’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. For full legal text, see our [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

---

## Account age eligibility

### Who can create a Penyzen account?

You must be at least 18 years of age to create a Penyzen account, start a campaign, or make a donation. We do not create accounts for anyone under 18.

### I am under 18. Can I use Penyzen?

No. If you are under 18, you cannot create an account or organize a campaign on Penyzen. The Platform is not intended for children under 13.

### Can I fundraise for my child or another minor?

Yes, but only a parent or legal guardian (who is at least 18) may organize and manage a campaign on behalf of someone under 18. The guardian must create and hold the Penyzen account in their own name. A minor cannot create or manage the account or campaign themselves.

### Can I donate if I am under 18?

No. Making a donation requires a registered account, and accounts are not created for anyone under 18.

---

## Organization and nonprofit accounts

### Is an EIN / Tax ID required for every organization?

No. An EIN / Tax ID is only requested for nonprofit (501(c)(3)) organizations during signup. Individual, for-profit, and other community group accounts do not provide an EIN.

### Does having an EIN make my campaign tax-deductible?

No. A donation is only potentially tax-deductible if the campaign is operated by or exclusively for the benefit of a verified 501(c)(3) organization and the donation is made to that organization. Penyzen does not provide tax advice.

## Privacy and minors

### Does Penyzen collect information from children?

The Platform is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, contact privacy@penyzen.com and we will delete it.

### How is information about a minor beneficiary handled?

If a campaign benefits a minor, a parent or legal guardian must be the account holder and organizer. Any information about the minor is provided by the guardian and handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
